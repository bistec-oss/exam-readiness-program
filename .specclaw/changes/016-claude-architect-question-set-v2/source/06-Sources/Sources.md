## Notes on source reliability
- Price ($99 vs $125) and validity period (6 vs 12 months) differ across secondary sources — **treat the official Skilljar registration page as the source of truth** and re-verify right before paying/registering.
- Community mock exams are **not** official Anthropic content — useful for pattern practice and pacing, but the official sample exam (via Anthropic Academy) should be your primary calibration tool.
