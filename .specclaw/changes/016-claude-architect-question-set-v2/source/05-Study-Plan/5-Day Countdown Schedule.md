# 5-Day Countdown Schedule

Full arc from today to exam day. Days 1-2 are the intensive study days detailed in [[2-Day Study Plan]]; days 3-5 taper into targeted review, spaced practice, and rest, which every pass report treats as essential (cramming past day 2 shows diminishing returns and hurts pacing/confidence on exam day).

| Day | Focus | Time | Key output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Domains 1–3 deep study + scenario drills | ~6-7h | [[2-Day Study Plan]] Day 1 table; "got wrong" list |
| **Day 2** | Domains 4–5 + full timed mock #1 | ~6-7h | [[2-Day Study Plan]] Day 2 table; domain-by-domain score breakdown |
| **Day 3** | Targeted weak-domain drilling | ~3h | Re-study your 1-2 weakest domains only; redo [[Anti-Patterns Cheatsheet]] cold as a self-quiz; full timed mock #2 in the afternoon |
| **Day 4** | Light review + integration | ~2h | Re-skim [[Exam Overview]] and [[Anti-Patterns Cheatsheet]] once; do a partial mock (20-30 questions) focused on remaining weak spots; **stop studying by early evening** |
| **Day 5 (exam day)** | Confidence pass only | ~30-45 min | Re-read [[Anti-Patterns Cheatsheet]] and [[Exam Overview]] "Core Mental Model" section once, morning of. No new material. Confirm logistics (see below). Take the exam. |

## Day 3 detail
1. Pull up your Day 1 + Day 2 "got wrong" notes and the domain breakdown from mock #1.
2. Spend ~70% of the 3 hours on your **two weakest domains** specifically — re-read the domain note, then redo every official + community-mock question tagged to that domain.
3. Do a fresh **full timed mock #2** in the afternoon. Compare domain breakdown to mock #1 — you want to see the weak domains close the gap. If a domain didn't improve, that's a signal to ask a specific "why does X beat Y here" question in your next Claude session rather than just re-reading.

## Day 4 detail
1. One clean pass over [[Exam Overview]] and [[Anti-Patterns Cheatsheet]] — this is your "shape of the exam" refresher, not new learning.
2. A shorter, partial mock (20-30 questions) targeting whatever was still weak after Day 3.
3. Deliberately **stop by early evening**. Sleep and pacing matter more than one more hour of cramming at this point — multiple pass reports flag fatigue/rushing as a bigger risk than knowledge gaps by this stage.

## Exam day (Day 5) checklist
- [ ] Confirm exam time, proctoring software (ProctorFree via Skilljar), and system requirements the night before — test your webcam/mic and close background apps in advance.
- [ ] Confirm current price/validity/retake policy on the [official Skilljar registration page](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/claude-certified-architect-foundations-certification) — reported figures vary across community sources (see [[Sources]]), don't rely on secondary numbers.
- [ ] It's **closed-book**: no Claude, no docs, no external tools during the exam — don't plan to look anything up.
- [ ] Quiet, private room; valid photo ID ready if required by the proctor.
- [ ] Morning-of: one light pass over [[Anti-Patterns Cheatsheet]] + [[Exam Overview]] core mental model. No new domains, no new mocks.
- [ ] During the exam: **answer every question** (no penalty for guessing, unanswered = wrong); flag hard ones and move on rather than grinding; watch the clock from question 1 (~2 min/question budget, 4 blocks of 15).
- [ ] Trust the reasoning pattern you drilled: deterministic > probabilistic when stakes are high; root-cause-with-least-infrastructure beats "add more stuff"; narrowest correct scope beats broadest.

## If you don't pass on the first attempt
Not covered in detail publicly — check the official Certification Exam Policy PDF (linked from the Skilljar registration page) for retake cooldown and fees before assuming anything. Several community sources note this information isn't consistently published, so don't guess.
